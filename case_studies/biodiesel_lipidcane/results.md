## Allocation of a shared impact to biodiesel
For an illustrative total system GWP of 40,000 kg CO2e/hr:

| Basis | Biodiesel's share | kg CO2e / kg biodiesel |
|-------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Mass | 43.3% | 1.953 |
| Energy (LHV) | 51.6% | 2.330 |
| Economic | 59.4% | 2.681 |

**Takeaway:** the biodiesel footprint changes by ~37% depending on allocation basis
alone — the basis must be reported, not assumed.
